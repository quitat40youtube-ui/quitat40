Video 3 — render the finished video (with voiceover)

Fetches all 179 scene images, plays your voiceover, and records the synced slideshow into one downloadable video file with the audio baked in. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

1. Choose your voiceover MP3 (the ElevenLabs… .mp3 file):

2. Click Prepare — decodes the audio and downloads the 179 images.

3. Click Render & Download. It records in real time (~20 min), so keep this tab open and in the foreground the whole time (background tabs throttle and corrupt the recording). You may mute the tab — audio is still captured. The video downloads automatically when done.

Idle. Choose an audio file, then Prepare.

Output format auto-selected: MP4 if your browser's recorder supports it (Safari), else WebM (Chrome/Firefox/Edge) — both upload to YouTube fine. Recommended: latest Chrome or Edge on desktop.
